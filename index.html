ICLR 2027  ·  Under Review  ·  Sole Author

Auditable, Not Opaque

A human-annotated benchmark for whether an LLM can verify safety compliance from real offshore documents (64 documents, 1,721 pages). Three frontier models reject false compliance claims 90.9 to 95.5% of the time, but recognise genuine compliance only 10.6 to 21.3% of the time, even though retrieval had already found the right evidence 81.9% of the time. Ground truth was labelled by one human with no AI involvement.
